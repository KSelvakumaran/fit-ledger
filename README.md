# Fit Ledger

A minimalist phone app (PWA) for daily wellbeing:

- Enter height and weight → BMI with Asian/Indian or WHO cut-offs and your healthy weight range
- Log food from a built-in list of ~80 common Indian foods (or add your own)
- Log gym sessions and activity → calories burnt
- Daily check: is what you ate helping or hurting? Score out of 100 plus specific suggestions
- Weight trend and 7-day summary
- Works offline; data stays on your phone (export a backup from the Me tab)

Calorie and nutrition values are estimates. Not medical advice.
